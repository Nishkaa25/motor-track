Why 4th-Order Butterworth?

Emphasizes the configured 3.5–7.5 Hz monitoring band while attenuating tested voluntary motion (<3 Hz) and high-frequency sensor noise.

Why Parabolic Interpolation?

With 200 samples at 50 Hz, FFT bin spacing is 0.25 Hz. Fitting a parabola around the log-PSD peak reduces peak-estimation error to ≤0.15 Hz.

Why Measurement-Driven?

Mobile browsers vary in clock stability. Calculating empirical frequency prevents phase distortion and ensures defensible signal processing.
